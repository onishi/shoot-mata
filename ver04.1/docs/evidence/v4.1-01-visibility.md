# v4.1-01 Combat Visibility Review

Date: 2026-11-01

## Change

- Moved the decorative foreground layer behind trails, ships, weakpoints, and projectiles. Background assets and simulation state are unchanged.
- Added local dark contrast fields and dashed shape outlines to the player, all 24 ordinary enemies, bosses, and hostile projectiles.
- Limited enemy, player, and projectile cue clearance to 5, 6, and 7 pixels. Collision radii and `ENEMY_VISUAL_CONTRACT` dimensions are unchanged.
- Made cue opacity and the invulnerable-player silhouette steady when reduced flash is enabled.

## Visual judgement

- The player now remains identifiable over the bright Stage 1 trace, Stage 4 fire, and Stage 6 cloud detail.
- Ordinary enemies have a shared salmon dashed contour, while hostile bullets retain circular/double-ring geometry. Decorative wind, rain, heat, and mirror lines remain thinner and do not use this contour.
- Boss-specific weakpoints remain gold or cyan and are not replaced by the generic boss outline.
- The Stage 6 reduced-flash capture retains the same local contrast without opacity cycling.
- No full-screen dimming or permanent labels were introduced.
