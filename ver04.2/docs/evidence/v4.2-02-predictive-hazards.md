# v4.2-02 Predictive Hazard Grammar

Date: 2026-11-02

## Change

Enemy hazards no longer converge on one double-circle mark. `sourceAttack` now selects one of four behavior-first silhouettes. Each silhouette combines a body shape and a direction cue, so color is supplementary rather than the only distinction.

| Silhouette | Static cues | Meaning | Regular enemy assignments |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| aimed | forward chevron + short single tail | track the present player position, then pass through | index-kite, cross-reference-pair, drip-scribe, ash-comma, mirror-mote |
| spread | diamond + forked upper/lower tails | leave the fan rather than only dodging its center | margin-skiff, folio-hauler, vane-scribe, gutter-frog, false-witness, null-choir |
| control | square + terminal crossbar | a lane, wall, or area is being occupied | eraser-mite, bracket-gull, tab-binder, basin-bell, kiln-censor, char-press, silver-obelisk, binding-wasp, archive-palanquin |
| sniper | narrow forward needle + long heavy tail | a fast, precise lane must be vacated | reed-needle, ember-courier, quotation-ram, revision-hound |

All 24 regular enemies are present in the assignment table, including eraser-mite whose direct trail-erasure action does not emit a bullet. All 20 named boss attack sources are mapped into the same four silhouettes. Special advocate embers, editor signature ink, and uncapturable mirror decoys retain their authored sprites or dashed treatment; capturable and uncapturable attacks therefore remain distinguishable without flash.

## Regression controls

- The simulation radius, velocity, spawn angle, cooldown, entity limit, collision logic, and AI input were not changed.
- ECHO/NORMAL weak-hazard recovery previously recognized only `enemy-volley`. It now recognizes the complete `enemy-*` family, preserving the prior recovery behavior after source names were split.
- Unit coverage asserts 24/24 regular enemy assignments, all four silhouettes, all named boss sources, and regular-hazard recovery classification.
- Autoplay passed 14/14 Stage, boss, MARGINALIA, and counterfactual reports.
- Formal campaign balance passed 90/90 runs with zero continues. Medians remained ECHO 23.37, NORMAL 23.47, PROOF 23.57 minutes; median damage remained 1 / 1 / 3. These values are identical to v4.2-01.
- Simulation performance passed at average / p95 / max 0.032 / 0.056 / 2.322 ms. Chrome rendering passed at 0.064 / 0.200 / 0.800 ms, 0 long tasks, and 2.43% heap growth.
- Production Chrome E2E passed 25/25, including reduced-flash and all required desktop resolutions.

## Image review

The v4.1 images were used as the before set and the regenerated v4.2 images as the after set. Stage 1 through 6 combat and set-piece images, all three phases of each boss, and the reduced-flash Stage 6 scene were inspected.

Observed differences:

- The old concentric-circle bullets gave no static clue about fan width or travel role. The new chevrons, diamonds, squares, and needles remain distinguishable in both bright Stage 2 and dark Stage 4/5 backgrounds.
- Directional tails rotate with velocity. Dense fans read as a formation while individual aimed and sniper shots still expose their travel line.
- The unchanged local halo keeps hazards separate from enemies and the cyan player/COLLATE wake. Square control shots are the widest new body, but remain inside the existing hazard clearance and do not obscure boss weakpoints.
- Reduced flash keeps the same steady high-contrast outline; no new cue depends on pulsing or blinking.

The comparison did not reveal a gameplay or legibility regression. The next improvement can proceed without changing this visual grammar.
