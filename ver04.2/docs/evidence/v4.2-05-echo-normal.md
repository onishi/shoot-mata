# v4.2-05 ECHO / NORMAL Learning Margin

## Change

- ECHO only: the first colliding hazard in each Stage becomes an `ECHO FORECAST` instead of damage.
- The forecast consumes that one hazard, gives 120 ticks (1 second) to evade, and then expires for the rest of the Stage.
- It does not clear the remaining field, grant permanent invulnerability, or change enemy HP, bullet speed, attack timing, hazard caps, or boss mechanics.
- NORMAL and PROOF use the unchanged collision path.
